# V1.12 Pilot source register (12-case pre-review)

This register describes a real candidate dossier, not an active benchmark.
Every `rights_decision`, historical label, and scenario-assumption review remains
empty until the local `benchmark-reviewer` completes an item-level review. A
policy-level licence is never treated as approval of third-party figures or
contributed content inside a publication.

The draft dossier is
`benchmarks/historical-benchmark.v1/work/pilot-curation-dossier.json`. It has
exactly 12 development cases, two per domain, and 24 distinct evidence URLs.

| Domain | Cases | Candidate material | Policy gate |
|---|---|---|---|
| Strait/security | `pilot_strait_01`, `pilot_strait_02` | EIA Japan briefs, EIA 2024 World Oil Transit Chokepoints, World Bank CMO October 2021 | EIA public-domain policy plus item-level third-party review; World Bank report-specific CC BY 3.0 IGO review |
| Energy | `pilot_energy_01`, `pilot_energy_02` | EIA AEO 2020/2022 and World Bank CMO October 2020/April 2023 | EIA public-domain policy plus item-level third-party review; World Bank report-specific CC BY 3.0 IGO review |
| Food | `pilot_food_01`, `pilot_food_02` | FAOSTAT October 2023 methodological releases and World Bank CMO April/October 2024 | FAOSTAT database terms (CC BY 4.0 by default) plus dataset/item review; World Bank report-specific review |
| Sanctions | `pilot_sanctions_01`, `pilot_sanctions_02` | OFAC 2021/2022 SDN change archives and World Bank CMO April 2022/October 2023 | U.S. government/public-domain treatment must be confirmed item by item; World Bank report-specific review |
| Trade | `pilot_trade_01`, `pilot_trade_02` | World Bank CMO October 2018/2019 and April 2020/2021 | Each report identifies CC BY 3.0 IGO but still requires third-party-content review |
| Finance | `pilot_finance_01`, `pilot_finance_02` | World Bank GEP January/June 2022, IDS 2022, and CMO October 2022 | Each publication and its selected locator require report-specific CC BY 3.0 IGO review |

Policy references checked for candidate preparation:

- [EIA copyright and reuse policy](https://www.eia.gov/about/copyrights_reuse.php)
- [FAO statistical database terms](https://www.fao.org/contact-us/terms/db-terms-of-use/en/)
- [World Bank public licences](https://datacatalog.worldbank.org/public-licenses)
- [Creative Commons BY 3.0 IGO](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/igo/)
- [U.S. Treasury copyright notice](https://home.treasury.gov/utility/copyright-and-other-notices)

The candidate `license_name`, coverage, locator, publication date, and scenario
mapping are review prompts. They are not findings. `finalize-pilot` will refuse
to create a Source Plan until all 24 rights decisions, all 12 development label
reviews, the frozen scenario rubric reasons, and the dossier hash are complete.

No raw source bytes, approval password, or unsealed historical label belongs in
Git. Source acquisition begins only after reviewer-authenticated finalization.
