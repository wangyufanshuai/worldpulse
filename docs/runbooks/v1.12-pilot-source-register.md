# V1.12 pilot source register (12-case pre-review)

This is a curation queue, not an active benchmark manifest. It records official
candidate material discovered for the six-domain pilot; it does not assert that
the material may be archived. A curator must record a positive license decision
and an input/outcome locator before placing an item in a source plan.

The register is intentionally not a `source-plan.json`: no case labels,
Evidence locator, reviewer identity or final archival license decision is
invented here. Once those fields are independently reviewed, the curator can
construct the 12-case pilot plan under
`benchmarks/historical-benchmark.v1/work/` and run the profile-specific CLI.

Policy-level findings recorded on 2026-07-17:

- [EIA's reuse policy](https://www.eia.gov/about/copyrights_reuse.php) states
  that its U.S. Government reports and data are public domain, while
  warning that third-party photographs and contributed material may remain
  protected. Each selected EIA item therefore still needs an item-level review.
- [World Bank open datasets](https://datacatalog.worldbank.org/public-licenses)
  default to CC BY 4.0 when the dataset metadata says so; catalog entries
  supplied by third parties must be reviewed separately.
- [FAO statistical databases](https://www.fao.org/contact-us/terms/db-terms-of-use/en/)
  default to CC BY 4.0 unless dataset metadata says otherwise and may contain
  third-party exceptions. This finding does not cover Food Outlook reports
  automatically.
- UNCTAD RMT, WTO publications and Federal Reserve/OFAC candidates remain
  `needs_review` until the exact item and its reuse basis are approved. They
  must not be represented as an approved `rights_review` merely because the
  publisher is an official institution.
