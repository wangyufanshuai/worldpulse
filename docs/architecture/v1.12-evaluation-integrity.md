# WorldPulse V1.12：评估完整性与历史盲测

V1.12 combines three validation tracks:

- Track A retains the 30 calibration cases for deterministic regression and Rule Pack promotion.
- Track B runs the 12 engineering cases as 84 production-path Mock members and recomputes hard gates from stored Artifacts.
- Track C runs 120 deterministic historical cases plus seven sealed cross-mode members for each of 30 blind cases, for 330 members.

The release coordinator therefore executes 414 Mock members. Historical direction, ranking and negotiation metrics are recorded as observation baselines and do not claim real-world predictive accuracy.

Historical suites cannot be activated until all official-source blobs exist and match their declared SHA-256. The repository intentionally contains no fabricated historical evidence.
